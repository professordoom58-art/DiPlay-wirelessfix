The Engineering Breakthrough

Most vehicle Android head units (including BYD DiLink, Geely, and generic units) lock down or lack the RFCOMM Bluetooth profile necessary to negotiate Apple CarPlay. Here is how DiPlay WirelessFix solves it:

📱

iPhone

Initiates standard wireless CarPlay link over Bluetooth LE.

Apple iOS Protocol
⚡

ESP32 Bridge

Handles the iAP2 RFCOMM connection and reports its Bluetooth MAC.

921,600 Baud USB UART
🚗

Android Head Unit

Receives high-throughput H.264 video and PCM audio over 5GHz Wi-Fi.

60 FPS Hardware Decoding

The ESP32 takes over only the low-bandwidth Bluetooth pairing handshake. Once connected, high-speed AirPlay video, maps, and audio stream directly through your vehicle's 5GHz Wi-Fi interface.

Up and Running in 3 Steps

Get behind the wheel with wireless CarPlay in minutes.

01

Install on Head Unit

Download the latest release APK directly to your car screen or transfer via flash drive. Grant requested permissions.

02

Plug In Your ESP32

Connect your ESP32 board to the car's USB media port. DiPlay automatically detects the bridge chip.

03

Connect & Drive

Turn on phone Bluetooth and tap Connect. Your iPhone pairs and launches CarPlay automatically every time you start the car.
