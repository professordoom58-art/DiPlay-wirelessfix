System Architecture

How the ESP32 Hardware Fix Works

Many automotive Android head units (including BYD DiLink and Geely/Lynk) ship with proprietary Bluetooth stacks that strictly lock down or reject the iAP2 RFCOMM connection needed by iOS for CarPlay pairing.

📱

1. iPhone Discovery

The iPhone advertises wireless CarPlay over standard Bluetooth LE and initiates the link request.

iOS iAP2 Protocol
⚡

2. ESP32 UART Bridge

The ESP32 microcontroller handles the low-bandwidth Bluetooth RFCOMM handshake over 921,600 baud USB serial.

Hardware-Assisted RFCOMM
🚗

3. High-Speed 5GHz Wi-Fi

Once paired, high-throughput H.264/HEVC video and audio stream directly via 5GHz Wi-Fi into the Android head unit.

60 FPS Hardware Decoding

Key Benefit: The microcontroller only handles the initial connection handshake. All heavy video, audio, and navigation data run directly through your vehicle’s high-speed Wi-Fi network with zero bottleneck.

Quickstart

Three Steps to Wireless CarPlay

Get up and running in minutes without taking apart your dashboard.

01

Install on Your Car

Download the latest DiPlay-latest.apk and install it on your Android head unit using a USB flash drive or your car's built-in file manager. Grant requested Bluetooth and Wi-Fi permissions.

02

Connect Your Hardware

Plug an ESP32 board (CP2102 or CH340) into the vehicle's USB media port for wireless pairing, or connect your iPhone directly via a certified USB data cable for wired CarPlay.

03

Pair Phone & Drive

Enable Bluetooth and Wi-Fi on your iPhone. Tap Connect inside DiPlay, accept the standard iOS CarPlay prompt, and the connection will launch automatically every time you start the engine.

Help & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to jailbreak my iPhone?

No. DiPlay operates as a standard, compliant Apple CarPlay receiver. Your iPhone will discover it natively just like any factory automotive CarPlay receiver.

Why is the ESP32 board needed?

Many Android car displays lack the Bluetooth RFCOMM profile needed to complete Apple's pairing handshake. The ESP32 provides this handshake over USB serial, leaving the high-speed video stream to your car's Wi-Fi.

How do I update an existing installation?

Simply download the new APK and install it directly over the existing app. The public signing key and all your configured preferences are automatically preserved.

Can I use a wired USB cable instead?

Yes. If you prefer a wired connection or do not have an ESP32 board, connect your iPhone with a USB data cable and select "Connect with USB" inside the application.

What should I do if the video display stutters?

In DiPlay Settings → Display, select 30 FPS, disable HEVC, and choose default icon size. Ensure your phone is connected to the vehicle's 5 GHz Wi-Fi hotspot rather than 2.4 GHz.

What permissions does DiPlay require?

DiPlay requests Bluetooth and Wi-Fi permissions to establish the connection, and Microphone permission if you use Siri or in-car phone calls.
